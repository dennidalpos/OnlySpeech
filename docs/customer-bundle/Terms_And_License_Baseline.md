# Terms And Software Use Baseline

## Scope

This is the repository-controlled baseline for direct desktop/B2B distribution of OnlySpeech.
It is intended to be externally readable as a seller-facing baseline, while still requiring final commercial review for any signed marketplace or customer contract.

## 1. Seller And Product

- Seller baseline: Danny Perondi
- Support channel baseline: <https://github.com/dennidalpos/OnlySpeech/issues>
- Product: OnlySpeech, a Windows desktop application for assisted in-person communication on one workstation with two display surfaces

## 2. License Grant

The seller grants the purchasing customer a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable right to install and use the delivered version of OnlySpeech on one Windows workstation at a time.
The delivered packaged build is intended to be activated with the customer email and activation code supplied after purchase for that licensed workstation scope.
A separate simultaneous workstation deployment requires a separate license and separate activation unless the seller agrees otherwise in writing.

## 3. Included Scope

The baseline commercial offer covers:

- the versioned packaged software delivered in the customer release bundle;
- the buyer-facing documentation copied into that bundle;
- best-effort support and fulfillment within the repository-controlled support policy.

The baseline commercial offer does not include hosted SaaS access, Azure Speech usage, OpenAI usage, or on-site commissioning services.

## 4. Customer-Provided Provider Accounts

The prudent baseline assumes the customer provides and controls its own credentials for the selected Azure Speech or OpenAI provider.
Unless a separate written agreement states otherwise, the software does not include bundled provider consumption or provider-account resale.

## 5. Restrictions

The customer must not:

- market the software as a standalone Azure or OpenAI resale service without an approved commercial model;
- use the software as the sole basis for critical, medical, legal, or safety decisions;
- remove required notices, attribution, or compliance artifacts from shipped builds when those artifacts are part of the delivered bundle;
- reuse or share activation material to enable additional simultaneous workstations outside the purchased license scope;
- claim that workstation-managed local TTS fallback is part of the approved commercial offer.

## 6. AI-Assisted Output

OnlySpeech is an AI-assisted communication aid.
Voice and transcript data are processed for the active turn, and outputs may be incomplete, incorrect, delayed, or unsuitable for high-risk reliance.

## 7. Customer Responsibilities

The customer remains responsible for:

- lawful collection and processing of voice and transcript data;
- notices and consent where required;
- provider-account administration and billing under the chosen model;
- retaining access to the purchased activation email and code for reinstall or support-led reactivation on the same licensed workstation;
- workstation hardening, local account controls, and physical deployment safeguards;
- deployment-specific legal, privacy, DPA, and retention obligations.

## 8. Support And Availability

Support scope, delivery contents, and update commitments follow the bundled **Support and Fulfillment Policy**.
The baseline support channel is the repository issue tracker.
No uptime SLA, hosted-service SLA, or emergency-response commitment is included in the repository-controlled offer.

## 9. Third-Party Components

OnlySpeech ships with third-party software and generated compliance artifacts.
The current product boundary is provider-owned Azure/OpenAI playback only and does not include a workstation-managed local TTS fallback offer.

## 10. Warranty And Liability

Except where a signed written agreement says otherwise, the repository-controlled baseline is delivered on an "as is" and "as available" basis, without a guarantee that the software is uninterrupted, error-free, or suitable for any high-risk use case.
The seller does not accept responsibility for provider outages, customer network conditions, customer hardware failures, or indirect or consequential losses arising from deployment-specific use.

## 11. Term And Termination

The customer may use the delivered version for the licensed workstation scope while the commercial relationship for that delivered version remains in force.
The seller may suspend or terminate support for material breach of these baseline terms, non-payment, or misuse outside the documented product boundary.
